HERO PHOTOS (optional)
----------------------
Put the photos you want featured in this folder (jpg / png). Any size, any aspect: each is cropped to a square.

* The first photos (sorted by file name: 01_..., 02_..., ...) are used for the framed photos that lift out of the
  mosaic next to each paragraph of the message. Tip: name them in the order you want them to appear.
* If you add more photos than the message has paragraphs, the extras replace some of the big showcase photos that fly
  past while the mosaic forms.
* If the folder is empty, or has fewer photos than needed, the rest are chosen automatically from your tile photos
  (sharp, well-lit photos with people in them).
